# Spirited Away
*Spirited Away is Japanese anime film that was writen and directed by Hayao Mizaki under Studio Ghibli in 2001. The film is about a 10 year old Chihiro who later gets the name "Sen" after she, along with her family, took a wrong turn while they were on their way to move residency. Long story short, they unknowingly wondered into the spirit world where different yokais live. Her parents turned into pigs which becomes the reason for her adventure. Along the way, she meets new friends who became catlysts for her maturity and quest towards escaping the spirit world.*
